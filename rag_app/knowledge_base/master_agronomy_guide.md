# ICAR Master Agronomy & Crop Cultivation Guide

This master knowledge base document contains comprehensive Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) agricultural guidelines, ideal micro-climate requirements, NPK fertilizer schedules, pest/disease prevention strategies, and irrigation management for all major Indian crop categories across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons.

---

## 1. Wheat Cultivation & Agronomy Guide (ICAR Standard)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Cool climate during vegetative growth (15°C to 20°C) and warm dry weather during ripening (22°C to 25°C). Frost during flowering causes grain sterility.
- **Rainfall**: 450mm to 650mm per season. Excess rainfall during harvest damages grain quality.
- **Relative Humidity**: 50% to 65% relative humidity. High humidity promotes rust diseases.
- **Soil pH**: Fertile, well-drained clay loams or loams with pH 6.0 to 7.5.

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 120 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 40 kg K2O per hectare (120:60:40).
- **Application Schedule**: Apply 50% N + 100% P + 100% K as basal dose at sowing. Top-dress remaining 50% N at first irrigation (CRI stage, 21 days after sowing).

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Yellow / Brown Rust**: Major fungal threat. Spray Propiconazole 25% EC (1ml/L) or Tebuconazole upon first appearance.
- **Loose Smut**: Treat seeds with Carboxin 75 WP (2g/kg seed) or Carbendazim before sowing.
- **Termites**: Treat soil with Chlorpyrifos 20% EC (2L/ha) with first irrigation.

### Irrigation Strategy
- **Critical Stages**: Crown Root Initiation (CRI - 21 DAS), Tillering (40 DAS), Jointing (60 DAS), Flowering (80 DAS), and Milk/Grain Filling stage (100 DAS).
- CRI stage irrigation is non-negotiable; missing it causes up to 30% yield loss.

## 2. Rice (Paddy) Cultivation & Agronomy Guide (ICAR Standard)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Optimal growing temperature is 22°C to 32°C. High temperatures above 35°C during flowering cause spikelet sterility.
- **Rainfall**: High water requirement ranging from 1000mm to 1500mm throughout the crop cycle. Requires flooded field conditions during early tillering.
- **Relative Humidity**: High relative humidity (70% - 85%) promotes vegetative growth.
- **Soil pH**: Slightly acidic to neutral soils (pH 5.5 to 7.0) with high clay content for water retention.

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 120 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 40 kg K2O per hectare (120:60:40).
- **Basal Application**: Apply 50% Nitrogen + 100% Phosphorus + 50% Potassium at the time of transplanting.
- **Top Dressing**: Apply remaining Nitrogen in two equal split doses at panicle initiation (30 days) and flowering stages.

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Blast Disease**: Fungal infection causing diamond-shaped lesions on leaves. Treat with Tricyclazole (0.6g/L) at first onset.
- **Stem Borer**: Apply Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% GR at 10 kg/ha during active tillering.
- **Bacterial Leaf Blight**: Spray Streptocycline (0.1g/L) + Copper Oxychloride (2.5g/L).

### Irrigation Strategy
- Maintain 3–5 cm water depth during transplanting and tillering stages.
- Drain field 10–15 days prior to harvest to promote uniform grain ripening.

## 3. Chickpea (Gram) Cultivation & Agronomy Guide (ICAR Standard)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Optimal temperature range is 15°C to 25°C. Highly sensitive to frost and extreme heat (>30°C during flowering causes flower drop).
- **Rainfall**: Low to moderate moisture requirement (450mm to 650mm). Extremely sensitive to waterlogging.
- **Relative Humidity**: Low to moderate humidity (50% - 65%). High humidity increases incidence of Ascochyta blight.
- **Soil pH**: Well-drained loamy to clay loam soils with pH 6.0 to 7.5.

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 20 kg N, 50 kg P2O5, 20 kg K2O per hectare (20:50:20). Chickpea is a pulse crop that fixes atmospheric nitrogen via Rhizobium root nodules.
- **Rhizobium Inoculation**: Seed treatment with Rhizobium culture (20g/kg seed) + PSB (Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria) enhances root nodulation and yield.

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Helicoverpa Pod Borer**: Major pest damaging pods. Install Pheromone traps (5/acre) and spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG (0.4g/L) at pod formation.
- **Wilt Disease**: Fungal root rot. Treat seeds with Trichoderma viride (4g/kg seed) before sowing.

### Irrigation Strategy
- Pre-sowing irrigation to ensure adequate seedbed moisture.
- Critical irrigation stages: 1. Pre-flowering stage (35–45 days after sowing), 2. Pod development stage (70–75 days after sowing). Avoid irrigation during active flowering.

## 4. Cotton Cultivation & Agronomy Guide (ICAR Standard)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Warm season crop. Requires 25°C to 35°C during vegetative growth and 21°C to 28°C during boll ripening. Minimum 180–200 frost-free days required.
- **Rainfall**: Requires 600mm to 1000mm rainfall. Sunny, dry weather during boll opening and harvesting is mandatory to preserve lint quality.
- **Relative Humidity**: 55% to 70% relative humidity.
- **Soil pH**: Deep black cotton soil (Vertisols) or alluvial soil with pH 6.5 to 8.0.

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 120 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 60 kg K2O per hectare (120:60:60) for Bt Cotton.
- **Micro-Nutrients**: Foliar spray of 1% Magnesium Sulphate + 1% 19:19:19 NPK at 45 and 65 days after sowing to prevent leaf reddening.

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Pink Bollworm**: Infests bolls and reduces lint quality. Hang PB Rope / Pheromone traps (10/acre) and spray Profenofos 50% EC (2ml/L).
- **Sucking Pests (Whitefly & Jassids)**: Spray Flonicamid 50% WG (0.3g/L) or Imidacloprid 17.8% SL.

### Irrigation Strategy
- Alternate furrow irrigation method recommended to conserve water.
- Critical irrigation stages: Square formation (45 days), Flowering stage (70 days), and Boll development stage (90 days). Avoid overhead irrigation during boll opening.

## 5. Maize (Corn) Cultivation & Agronomy Guide (ICAR Standard)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Optimal range is 20°C to 30°C. Cold temperatures below 10°C retard germination and vegetative development.
- **Rainfall**: Moderate to high requirement (600mm to 900mm) evenly distributed throughout the growing season.
- **Relative Humidity**: 60% to 75% relative humidity is ideal for tasseling and silking.
- **Soil pH**: Deep, fertile, well-drained loamy soils with pH 5.5 to 7.5. Sensitive to salinity and waterlogging.

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 120 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 50 kg K2O per hectare (120:60:50).
- **Application Schedule**: Apply 33% N + 100% P + 100% K as basal dose. Apply remaining N in two top-dressings at knee-high stage (25 days) and silking stage (45 days).

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Fall Armyworm (FAW)**: Devastating pest. Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG (0.4g/L) or Spinetoram 11.7% SC (0.5ml/L) inside the whorl.
- **Turcicum Leaf Blight**: Apply Mancozeb (2.5g/L) at first symptom onset.

### Irrigation Strategy
- Critical moisture stages: Knee-high stage, Tasseling stage, and Silking/Cob formation stage.
- Water deficiency during silking reduces kernel filling by up to 50%.

## 6. Soybean Cultivation & Agronomy Guide (ICAR Standard)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Warm conditions between 25°C and 32°C. Temperatures above 38°C during flowering cause floral abortion.
- **Rainfall**: 750mm to 900mm well-distributed during Kharif season. Sensitive to both drought and waterlogging.
- **Relative Humidity**: 65% to 75%.
- **Soil pH**: Fertile loams and vertisols (black soils) with pH 6.3 to 7.5.

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 20 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 40 kg K2O, 20 kg S per hectare (20:60:40:20).
- **Bio-fertilizer**: Inoculate seed with *Bradyrhizobium japonicum* and PSB (Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria) at 5g/kg seed.

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV)**: Transmitted by whitefly. Control vector using Thiamethoxam 25% WG (100g/ha).
- **Girdle Beetle**: Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (150ml/ha) at 30–35 days after sowing.

### Irrigation Strategy
- Primarily rainfed. If dry spells occur, supply protective irrigation at flowering and pod filling stages.

## 7. Sugarcane Cultivation & Agronomy Guide (ICAR Standard)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Tropical crop requiring high temperatures (28°C to 38°C) during germination and growth, and cool dry weather (18°C to 24°C) for ripening and sucrose accumulation.
- **Rainfall**: High requirement (1200mm to 1800mm) distributed throughout the 10–12 month growth cycle.
- **Relative Humidity**: 70% to 85% relative humidity.
- **Soil pH**: Deep, well-drained loams and heavy black soils with pH 6.5 to 7.8.

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 250 kg N, 115 kg P2O5, 115 kg K2O per hectare (250:115:115).
- **Application**: Apply 25% N + 100% P + 50% K basal at planting; top-dress remaining N in three splits at 45, 90, and 120 days.

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Red Rot Disease**: Major fungal threat. Use disease-free seed setts treated with Carbendazim (1g/L).
- **Early Shoot Borer**: Apply Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% GR (18 kg/ha) at planting.

## 8. Bajra (Pearl Millet), Jowar (Sorghum) & Ragi (Millets Guide)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: High temperature tolerance (28°C to 38°C). Thrives in arid and semi-arid regions.
- **Rainfall**: Very low to low moisture requirement (300mm to 500mm). Highly drought-resilient.
- **Relative Humidity**: 40% to 55%.
- **Soil pH**: Sandy loams, light red soils, and alkaline soils with pH 6.8 to 8.2.

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 80 kg N, 40 kg P2O5, 40 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Bio-fertilizer**: Azospirillum and PSB seed inoculation (20g/kg seed).

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Downy Mildew / Green Ear**: Seed treatment with Metalaxyl 35 SD (6g/kg seed).
- **Shoot Fly**: Early sowing within 7–10 days of monsoon onset; apply Phorate 10G or Carbofuran at sowing.

---

## 9. Groundnut (Peanut), Sunflower & Sesamum (Oilseeds Guide)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Warm temperature requirement (25°C to 32°C). Soil temperature above 20°C required for peg development.
- **Rainfall**: 500mm to 700mm well-distributed. Dry weather during maturity and harvesting.
- **Relative Humidity**: 55% to 70%.
- **Soil pH**: Well-drained sandy loams rich in Calcium & Organic matter with pH 6.0 to 7.5.

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 25 kg N, 50 kg P2O5, 75 kg K2O + 200 kg Gypsum/ha at peg initiation stage (35–40 DAS).
- **Calcium Impact**: Gypsum supplies essential Calcium for pod filling and prevents empty shell (pops) syndrome.

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Tikka Leaf Spot**: Spray Mancozeb 75 WP (2g/L) or Carbendazim (1g/L) at 40 and 60 DAS.
- **White Grub**: Treat seeds with Chlorpyrifos 20 EC (25ml/kg seed) prior to sowing.

---

## 10. Mustard & Rapeseed Cultivation Guide

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Cool climate crop (15°C to 25°C) grown during Rabi season. Frost at flowering causes severe pod loss.
- **Rainfall**: Low moisture requirement (350mm to 500mm).
- **Soil pH**: Loam and sandy loam soils with pH 6.5 to 7.8.

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 80 kg N, 40 kg P2O5, 40 kg K2O + 40 kg Elemental Sulphur per hectare.
- **Sulphur Impact**: Sulphur increases oil content by 2% to 4% in mustard seeds.

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Mustard Aphids**: Sucking pest damaging inflorescence. Spray Dimethoate 30 EC (1.7ml/L) or Thiamethoxam 25 WG.
- **Alternaria Blight**: Spray Mancozeb (2.5g/L) at 45 and 60 DAS.

---

## 11. Potato, Onion & Garlic Cultivation Guide

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Cool night temperatures (15°C to 20°C) essential for tuberization / bulb development. High temperatures (>30°C) inhibit tuber initiation.
- **Rainfall**: 450mm to 650mm evenly distributed or controlled via micro-irrigation.
- **Soil pH**: Well-drained friable sandy loams with pH 5.5 to 6.8 (Potato prefers slightly acidic soil to prevent Scab).

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 150 kg N, 80 kg P2O5, 100 kg K2O per hectare (Potato/Onion).
- **Potash Application**: High Potassium requirement for starch synthesis and bulb storage stability.

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Late Blight of Potato**: Devastating fungal disease. Spray Cymoxanil + Mancozeb (2g/L) or Metalaxyl + Mancozeb.
- **Purple Blotch of Onion**: Spray Mancozeb (2.5g/L) + Sticker.

---

## 12. Ginger, Turmeric & Commercial Spices Cultivation Guide

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Warm, humid tropical climate (25°C to 35°C). Thrives under partial shade.
- **Rainfall**: High rainfall requirement (1500mm to 2500mm). Highly susceptible to rhizome rot under waterlogging.
- **Soil pH**: Rich friable loams or red soils with high organic matter and pH 5.8 to 6.5.

### Nutrient Management (NPK Ratios)
- **Recommended NPK**: 75 kg N, 50 kg P2O5, 50 kg K2O + 25 tonnes FYM per hectare.
- **Mulching**: Apply green leaf mulching (12 t/ha) at planting and 90 days to conserve moisture and suppress weeds.

### Disease & Pest Management
- **Rhizome Rot**: Soil drenching with Copper Oxychloride (3g/L) or Trichoderma viride application.
- **Shoot Borer**: Spray Chlorantraniliprole (0.3ml/L).

## 13. Jute, Tobacco & Commercial Cash Crops Guide

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: High temperature (28°C to 38°C) and high relative humidity (75% - 90%).
- **Rainfall**: Heavy rainfall (1200mm to 1800mm) during growing season.
- **Soil pH**: Fertile alluvial soils with pH 6.0 to 7.2.

### Agronomy & Nutrient Management
- **Recommended NPK**: 80 kg N, 40 kg P2O5, 40 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Harvesting Stage**: Harvest jute at small pod stage (120–135 days) for optimum fiber strength and quality.

---

## 14. Pulses Guide (Pigeonpea/Arhar, Green Gram/Moong, Black Gram/Urad, Lentil)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: 20°C to 30°C optimal. Highly resilient to temperature fluctuations.
- **Rainfall**: Low to moderate requirement (400mm to 750mm).
- **Soil pH**: Neutral, well-drained soils with pH 6.2 to 7.8.

### Bio-Fertilizer & Nutrition
- **NPK**: 20 kg N, 50 kg P2O5, 20 kg K2O + Rhizobium & PSB seed treatment.
- Pulses enrich soil fertility through root nodule biological nitrogen fixation (30–40 kg N/ha saved for next crop).

---

## 15. Vegetables & Horticulture Fruits Guide (Tomato, Chilli, Mango, Banana, Citrus, Papaya)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: 20°C to 32°C. Sensitive to extreme frost and heat waves.
- **Irrigation**: Micro-drip irrigation with fertigation schedules optimizes yield by up to 35%.
- **Soil pH**: Rich well-drained loams with pH 6.0 to 7.2.

### Plant Protection
- **Fruit Borer / Leaf Curl Virus**: Control vector (whitefly/thrips) with Imidacloprid (0.5ml/L) and install sticky traps.

---

## 16. Plantation Crops Guide (Coconut, Cashewnut, Tea, Coffee, Rubber)

### Ideal Growth Conditions
- **Temperature**: Tropical humid climate (22°C to 34°C).
- **Rainfall**: High annual rainfall (1500mm to 3000mm).
- **Soil pH**: Laterite, red loams, and coastal alluvial soils with pH 5.2 to 6.8.

### Management
- Apply organic basin mulching, green manuring, and balanced NPK fertigation schedule.
